## Takeaways

- **Pass-rate separation:** brownfield still shows **single FAIL / team PASS**; Commit0 `tinydb` does not (near-ceiling 199/201 on continuous and team).
- **Context modes:** on `tinydb`, **continuous ≈ team** on score; fresh arm did not finish — not yet comparable.
